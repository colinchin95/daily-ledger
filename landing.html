Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Expense tracking, income tracking, budgets, sub-categories, trends and backups are free. Pro adds unlimited AI and removes ads.

How does Back Tap work?

RichAuntie adds a ready-made shortcut in one tap. Pick it in Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap, then double-tap the back of your iPhone on any payment screen — e-wallet, DuitNow, bank app or e-receipt.

Which banks work?

Any bank or credit card that gives you a CSV, PDF or a readable screenshot — Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank, RHB, Hong Leong and more.

Where is my data?

On your device. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on encrypted sync or choose to scan a receipt or statement.
